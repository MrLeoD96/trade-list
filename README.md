### My Rules

to ensure trading is well enough and fun, there's a couple of very universal rules.

1. **Politeness & Patience:** i've got a life outside of trading as i'm sure you do. please allow up to a few days for a response from me. still, if i somehow genuinely missed your message, feel free to poke me because i really am that forgetful and never mean harm :)
2. **Trading Ratio:** a standard ratio of **1:1** (one video/audio for one video/audio). i don't do special ratios for "rare" or new releases. if you want more stuff from my list than i want from yours, just ask and i'll probably just gift some things.
3. **NFT:** i strictly and wholeheartedly respect NFT dates. if an item is NFT until a certain date, it will *not* be traded until that date passes. also do not trade with me your items that are NFT. masters are incredible and we should be thanking them 24/7. this applies to rules like some masters only traded via email.
4. **No Selling:** i do not make bootlegs. i do not sell bootlegs. i only trade. if you are looking to buy, you may visit a master's official site.
5. **Format:** I trade mostly via Mega, as well as some Google Drive/Dropbox/Transfer links. this will be listed with the items. make sure your links are usable and lmk if any of the links i provide are broken and i will soon fix it <3
6. **Be Cool and Chill and Stuff:** this is bootleg trading let's all just have a good time xx
