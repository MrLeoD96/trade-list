# Rules

## My Rules
### *buy me a dinner first, won't ya?*

to ensure trading is well enough and fun, I've a couple of very basic universal rules:

---

1. **Politeness & Patience:** listen, i've got a life outside of trading as i'm sure you do too. please allow up to a few days for a response from me. still, if i somehow genuinely missed your message, feel free to poke me because i really am that forgetful and i never mean harm :)
2. **Trading Ratio:** a standard ratio of **1:1** (one video/audio for one video/audio) or **2:1** (two audios for one video). i don't do special ratios for "rare" or new releases. if you want more stuff from my list than i want from yours, just ask and i'll probably just gift some things. i generally gift up to 3 items.
3. **NFT:** i strictly and wholeheartedly respect NFT dates. if an item is NFT until a certain date, it will *not* be traded until that date passes. also do not trade with me your items that are NFT. masters are incredible and we should be thanking them 24/7.
4. **No Selling:** i do not make bootlegs. i do not sell bootlegs. i only trade. if you are looking to buy, you may visit a master's official platform. i will happily point you in that direction :)
5. **Format:** I trade mostly via Mega, as well as some Google Drive/Dropbox/Transfer links that i've yet to convert. if you can't use one of those platforms lmk and i'll reupload to Mega. please make sure your links are usable and tell me if any of the links i provide are broken and i will swiftly fix it <3
6. **Be Cool and Chill and Stuff:** this is bootleg trading let's all just have a good time xx
